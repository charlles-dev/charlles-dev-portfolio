# Charlles.dev identity

## Thesis

A cinematic, editorial developer portfolio where a tactile character introduces a real engineering practice. It should feel authored, curious, technically credible, and slightly playful.

## Keep

- Near-black canvas, warm white text, and acid green as a precise signal rather than ambient decoration.
- Space Grotesk for expressive editorial type and JetBrains Mono for metadata, proof, routes, and technical detail.
- The Charlles character and video hero as the main visual signature.
- Evidence-first project narratives: problem, decision, next step, public link.
- Direct Brazilian Portuguese voice, with equivalent intent in English and Spanish.

## Avoid

- Generic bento grids, glass cards, purple/cyan gradients, floating pills, empty metrics, or dashboard chrome.
- Repeating the same eyebrow/title/body/card pattern on every route without a page-specific composition.
- Claims such as “innovative”, “powerful”, “seamless”, or “world-class” without proof.
- Multiple accent colors or a green glow applied to every interactive element.
- Decorative motion on mount, endless loops outside the hero, and effects that compete with reading.

## Page roles

- Home: establish identity, competence, and the fastest route to work.
- Work panel: turn projects into proof of judgment, not a repository inventory.
- About panel: explain working principles with a human voice.
- Contact panel: reduce the effort needed to start a useful conversation.
- Not found: recover orientation while keeping the character and tone.

Keep current interests, working process, and engineering decisions inside project narratives or the About panel when they add evidence. Do not create standalone Now, Process, or Engineering routes: they make this portfolio read like a product or documentation site.
